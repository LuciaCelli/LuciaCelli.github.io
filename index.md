I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the **University of Luxembourg**, where I work under the supervision of Professor [Giovanni Peccati](https://sites.google.com/site/giovannipeccati/home) on the mathematical aspects of neural networks.

Previously, I obtained a Master's degree in Mathematics from the **University of Rome Tor Vergata**, where I worked on my thesis under the supervision of Professor [Domenico Marinucci](https://sites.google.com/view/domenicomarinucci/). I completed my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics at the **University of Perugia**.

---
## Research Interests

- Theoretical aspects of neural networks
- Neural network approximation at initialization
- Bayesian neural networks
- Convergence analysis and approximation theory
- Edgeworth expansions and asymptotic analysis
- Wide neural networks and kernel methods
- Stein's Method

- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Neural network approximation at initialization and in Bayesian settings
- 📚 **Institution:** University of Luxembourg
- 👨‍🏫 **Advisor:** Prof. Giovanni Peccati
- 💡 Passionate about rigorous mathematical analysis in deep learning
- 🤝 Open to collaboration and interdisciplinary research
- 📖 Lifelong learner with a commitment to scientific excellence
